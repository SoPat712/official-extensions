# Yandex Favicons

Gets icons from Yandex's favicon service, at 120px when degoog wants more than 32px:

```
https://favicon.yandex.net/favicon/v2/<host>?size=120
```

No account or key needed.

## Privacy

Yandex sees every domain in your results. The requests come from your server's IP, or from the proxy or transport you set for this provider. Your visitors and their queries never reach Yandex, but a burst of domains arriving together still hints at what someone searched.

Yandex is a Russian company and falls under Russian law.
